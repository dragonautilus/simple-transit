Simple public transit puts public transport on your Even G2, wherever Transitous has timetable data.

- Departure board of the stops closest to you: minutes to departure, line, destination, platform and delay. Swipe to switch between nearby stops. A small street map shows where the stop is in relation to you, with distance, direction and walking time. Where departures leave from both sides of a street, the map marks each side with the same letter as the departures.
- Preset targets (home, work, any stop or address), managed on the phone.
- Networks: Transitous (international) is preferred; in Switzerland, transport.opendata.ch takes over when Transitous is unavailable. Either can also be set by hand.
- Pick a connection and follow it on the glasses: a journey line with a dot showing how far along you are, driven by the real-time timetable and refined by GPS.
- Every change with arrival and departure time and platform, platform changes and delays highlighted.
- Check in to a journey: close the app during a long ride and it opens straight into your journey again. Checked out automatically after arrival.

Timetable data: Transitous and its data sources (https://transitous.org/sources/), incl. DELFI e.V. (CC BY 4.0). Swiss fallback: opentransportdata.swiss via transport.opendata.ch. Map: © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). All sources and licences are listed in the app under "Data sources & licences".
