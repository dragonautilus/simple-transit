Simple euro transit puts public transport in Switzerland and Germany on your Even G2.

- Departure board of the stops closest to you: minutes to departure, line, destination, platform and delay. Swipe to switch between nearby stops. A small street map shows where the stop is in relation to you, with distance, direction and walking time.
- Preset targets (home, work, any stop or address), managed on the phone.
- Networks: Switzerland (transport.opendata.ch) and Germany & Europe (Transitous), picked automatically by location or set by hand.
- Pick a connection and follow it on the glasses: a journey line with a dot showing how far along you are, driven by the real-time timetable and refined by GPS.
- Every change with arrival and departure time and platform, platform changes and delays highlighted.
- Check in to a journey: close the app during a long ride and it opens straight into your journey again. Checked out automatically after arrival.

Timetable data: Switzerland: opentransportdata.swiss via transport.opendata.ch. Germany & Europe: Transitous and its data sources (https://transitous.org/sources/), incl. DELFI e.V. (CC BY 4.0).
